About

Uniquefacturing (UFAC) turns ideas into working products. One engineer takes responsibility end to end, from circuit design and firmware to the software solution that runs on top of it.

Having delivered product development projects across several industries, UFAC now concentrates on solutions built on computer vision and wireless communication.
